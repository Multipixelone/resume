The floor is exactly $100,000, so it does not strictly satisfy above $100,000. Its build-with-AI, explain-it-on-camera, teach-the-workflow combination is worth considering if the employer can meet the salary target. ## What to show in applications

Use the existing communications/systems material as a factual starting point, then choose evidence by role. For Sent, lead with the public Fluso skills, brand validator, and one working demo whose implementation ownership is clear. For Socket, lead with actual published videos, audio craft, and a concise explanation of editorial choices. For Notion, show a useful developer guide/demo and public speaking strength; do not invent community traction. For Tenex, a narrated workflow walkthrough can connect AI use to concrete outputs, but it does not replace the requested professional tenure.

Keep preparation bounded: first assemble a reusable small portfolio from existing public work, then tailor the opening and evidence for each job. Do not build a large unpaid speculative project for every posting. Private Prem repository contents should not be published to create samples. Follow [REFERENCE.md](../REFERENCE.md) on what can be claimed and linked.
