## Work and desired experience

This position covers the design, implementation, enhancement, maintenance, and analysis of software systems. The posting describes highly technical or supervisory work across database management, operating systems, data communications, applications, and related software functions. Staff may perform duties of lower titles and assignment levels when needed.

- **Cloud Administrator track:** Desired knowledge of Microsoft Azure, AWS, GCP, Docker, Kubernetes, PowerShell, Azure Monitor, CloudWatch, and Blob.
- **VMware Engineer track:** Desired knowledge of VMware, vSphere, vCenter, ESXi, PowerCLI, Ansible, Veeam, VLANs, TCP/IP, and DNS.

## Assignment levels

- **I:** Under supervision, with considerable latitude for independent action or judgment. Designs, implements, enhances, and maintains software systems; may supervise a unit or independently perform highly complex technical work.
- **II:** Under general supervision, with wide latitude for independent initiative and judgment. May supervise difficult systems development work, serve as project leader on highly complex systems analysis and development, or supervise multiple applications programming units.
- **III:** Under general supervision, with broad scope for independent initiative and judgment. Directs systems development and applications programming units, or coordinates complex integrated software projects in an online, communications-based environment.
- **IV:** Under minimum direction, with the broadest scope for independent initiative and judgment. Acts as a senior technical authority on extremely complex, ongoing software functions; coordinates multiple projects of extraordinary complexity and major data center or distributed processing undertakings. May supervise units. See the additional qualification below.

## Minimum qualifications

The posting gives three routes:

1. A bachelor's degree from an accredited college, including or supplemented by 24 semester credits in computer science or a related computer field, plus two years of satisfactory full-time computer software experience in systems development and analysis, applications programming, database administration, systems programming, or data communications. One year must be as a project leader or major contributor on a complex project.
2. A four-year high school diploma or equivalent, plus six years of the same satisfactory full-time computer software experience, including one year as a project leader or major contributor on a complex project.
3. Equivalent education and/or experience. For route 2, accredited college education may replace up to two years of required experience at 60 semester credits per year. A master's degree in computer science or a related computer field may replace one year of required experience under route 1 or 2.

Every candidate must have at least a four-year high school diploma or equivalent and at least one year of satisfactory full-time experience as a project leader or major contributor on a complex project.

**Level IV special note:** In addition to the requirements above, placement at Assignment Level IV requires one year of satisfactory experience as a project leader or major contributor on a complex project in data administration, database management systems, operating systems, data communications systems, capacity planning, and/or online applications programming.
