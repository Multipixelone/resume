# Resume and Business Cards

Tailored resumes and the physical business cards handed out alongside them.

## Language

**Resume variant**:
A version of a resume tailored to a particular audience or opportunity.
_Avoid_: Card profile

**Card profile**:
An audience-specific identity and set of contact details for a business card. Multiple resume variants can share one card profile.
_Avoid_: Resume variant, per-resume card

**Business card**:
A physical card representing a card profile, with printable artwork and an image representation of that artwork.
_Avoid_: vCard, contact page
